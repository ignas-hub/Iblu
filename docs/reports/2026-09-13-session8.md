# Session 8 — the Gain layer

Run 2026-09-13 evening against `docs/plans/2026-09-13-sessions5-8.md` §4.

Source material: the gain-practice rules (`315ccb68`), the historical gains
register (`ad665962`), and the seven baselines. Iblu measures; it never grades.
No streaks, no targets on the practice itself.

## What exists now that did not

**"What moved today?" is a card (2026-09-13).** One per evening, multi-tap,
and — unlike every other question — *not* a supersede chain: each tap writes its
own `work_log` entry tagged `gain` with `meta.kind` of `learned`, `progressed`
or `experienced`. Three things happening in one day are three gains, not one
answer revised twice.

Options are composed from evidence that already happened, and a validator
rejects any option that is a plan, a percentage, future tense, or a goal not yet
reached — as a plain function *and* as a Pydantic validator on `Option`, so a
bad option fails schema validation and drops the whole set to the deterministic
fallback rather than reaching him.

**A quiet day still gets asked.** The first version returned no card when IBLU
had seen no evidence — which meant the practice would fire least on exactly the
days it is for. IBLU can only observe mail, Chat and calendar; most of what
moves a day is none of those. The card now appears with "Add one → reply" as its
only option. IBLU still invents nothing; it just asks.

**Body and mind are two numbers.** Four composed options mapping to `body` and
`mind` on a 1–5 scale, plus a reply of the form `body 1 mind 3` that overrides
with exact values. Stored as `work_log` tagged `health`. The review will report
weekly averages and the backward delta from the 2026-09-13 baseline — numbers
only. No mood inference, no interpretation, no advice. That restraint is the
feature.

**The week's own language is audited.** Over Ignas's *own* sent text only, the
Truth section counts Gap phrasings — "not fast enough", "should have",
"behind", superlatives, comparisons against others — and quotes at most three of
his own lines, fifteen words each. Regex only; the plan allows an LLM to
confirm, but a false positive here costs more than a miss, because quoting his
words back at him for a phrase he did not mean is precisely the grading posture
IBLU exists to remove. `behind the scenes` passes, deliberately. This week: 78
messages scanned, zero findings.

**A goal written as a Gap says so.** `context_log` returns a `gap_warning` on a
decision or preference containing a superlative, a comparison to another
company, a race, an obligation or a distance from an ideal — and on anything
tagged `priority` that names no observable end state. **The entry is always
written first.** A warning that could block would make IBLU a grader.

**The monthly statement rides the weekly review.** On the last Friday of each
month the review carries a fourth section: per venture, the 2026-09-13
baseline set against what is dated since. No adjectives, and no timer of its own
— one fewer unit to forget, landing where he already reads on a Friday evening.
A venture with nothing listed says so in those words: *nothing recorded since —
which is not nothing happening.*

**Every LLM call carries the rule.** Composer, review and judge all take:
measure backward from the baselines, never against an ideal, a goal, a
competitor or another person; state gains as dated evidence; never praise
generically; never present a plan as a gain; gains first.

**The mission counts gains on a cadence** — 24 h, 7 d, 30 d, 90 d, 12 m,
measured backward from the 2026-09-13 baselines, with an initiative reaching
`autonomous` named as the largest gain.

## What the repo contradicted

- **Nothing structural.** `blocks` already supported `confidence='fact'` and
  `source='ping'`, so §4 needed no migration.
- **One self-collision worth recording:** the plan's own required phrasing for
  an empty removal — "not enough evidence" — trips the validator's own
  `not enough` rule. It says "insufficient evidence" instead.
- **Two bugs the same shape, found by rendering it for real.** The weekly
  review counted the priorities Ignas wrote today as gains, and so did the
  evening card ("Learned: YEARLY TOP PRIORITY — Jakusi"). A priority, a
  baseline and the gain rules are the measuring stick, not progress against it.
  Both queries now exclude them, along with superseded rows and test entries.

## Simplifications, stated plainly

- **"Progressed" evidence only checks `fact` blocks** on projects belonging to a
  venture with a current priority. Thread closure and commits are not yet
  observable as a signal, so they cannot be offered.
- **Gains and body/mind cards are always deterministic**, never LLM-composed.
  The §4.6 rule is in the composer's system prompt regardless.
- **A non-body-mind reply to an evening ping is read as a gain**, because the
  delivery model is one thread per ping, not one per card.
- **The "share of attention serving each venture's priority"** metric (§1.7,
  LLM-judged `serves` / `neutral` / `against`) is not built. It needs
  per-signal priority judgement; the three required Truth sections came first.
  Listed here rather than quietly dropped.
