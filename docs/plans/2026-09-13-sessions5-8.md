### 4.1 Evening gains card (1 card, up to 3 taps, each tap independent — not a supersede chain)
Question kind `gains`, `multi=true`: header `"What moved today?"`; 3–4 pre-composed options from evidence — **learned** (a decision or correction logged today), **progressed** (a thread closed, a commit, a stage moved, a `fact` block on a priority's project), **experienced** (a `present` family or life block) — plus `Add one → reply`. Each tap writes `work_log` tagged `gain` with `meta.kind ∈ {learned, progressed, experienced}` and the evidence ids. Thread replies to this card are `gain` entries too. **Validation:** an option is rejected if it is a plan, a percentage, future tense, or a goal not yet reached (regex list + LLM instruction; the historical register shows exactly this drift). Only things that already happened.

### 4.2 Body / mind card (1 card, 1 tap)
Header `"Today — body / mind"`, four composed options `strong · excited`, `strong · tired`, `weak · excited`, `weak · exhausted` (mapping to body ∈ {4,2}, mind ∈ {4,2} on the 1–5 scale; a thread reply `body 1 mind 3` overrides with exact values), plus escape. Stored as `work_log` tagged `health`, `meta={body, mind}`. The review reports weekly averages and the backward delta from the baseline (body 1, mind 2 on 2026-09-13) — numbers only, never an interpretation of mood, never a diagnosis, never advice.

### 4.3 Gap-language audit (weekly, inside the review's Truth section)
Over Ignas's own sent text for the week (signals where `actor='me'`): count Gap phrasings (`not fast enough`, `should have`, `behind`, `still not`, comparatives against others, `best`/`most` framings) — regex first, LLM to confirm; quote at most three of his own lines, ≤15 words each, next to the same week's gains. His words only, never others'.

### 4.4 Gap-goal check in `context_log` (warn, never block)
When a `decision` or `preference` entry is written with tag `priority` or content matching superlatives/comparisons against others, the tool response includes `gap_warning` with a one-line backward-measurable rewrite suggestion. It never rejects the write.

### 4.5 Monthly backward statement (no new timer)
